# Corpus sources and credits

The harness and Christopher Albert's derivations are distributed under the
[MIT licence](LICENSE). Contributed originals are credited separately below;
permission to include a script does not establish an unrecorded original
licence or exclusive authorship. A collection name identifies the source of a
derivation, not a licence for the whole project from which it was collected.
Generated SymPy companions follow the source derivations and are not independent
sources.

## Contributed derivations

Verena Eslbauer contributed `onesizefitsall`, associated with her bachelor's
thesis, *Two analytical solutions to the Grad-Shafranov equation using
Solov'ev pressure and poloidal current profiles*, Graz University of Technology,
20 November 2017 ([thesis](https://itp.tugraz.at/Lokales-TU/Arbeiten/Bakk/Eslbauer_Verena_-__Two_analytical_solutions_to_the_Grad-Shafranov_equation_using_Solovev_pressure_and_poloidal_current_profiles_-_2018-01-02.pdf)).
The public filename dates the deposit to January 2018. The corpus maintainer
approved retaining this credited contribution; an original licence or separate
contributor permission is not recorded. Other polynomial
Grad-Shafranov derivations in this corpus are Christopher Albert's input to
the teaching project.

Philipp Ulbl supplied the five scripts in `gh-itpplasma-PhilippsLegacy` from
his notes and tearing-mode code. His master's thesis is *Effects of Resonant
Magnetic Perturbations on the Suppression of Edge Localized Modes*, Technical
Physics, Graz University of Technology, March 2020
([library record](https://diglib.tugraz.at/effects-of-resonant-magnetic-perturbations-on-the-suppression-of-edge-localized-modes-2020)).
The source collection identifies the tearing-mode code as his thesis work;
it does not establish exclusive authorship of every notebook derivation.
The collection was handed over to the research group with permission for use
and publication.

The KiLCA derivations are retained with permission confirmed by the corpus
maintainer and are credited to the KiLCA contributors. Individual notebook
authors and an original source licence are not recorded. Neither their
presence here nor a licence on another KiLCA variant supplies those facts.

Bernhard Schnizer taught the symbolic methods behind the course notebooks in
`archive-tu` and `archive-old`. Christopher Albert gratefully acknowledges
that teaching and the course material on which his notebooks build.

The original collections of the contributed scripts do not record per-file
reuse terms. The corpus MIT notice applies to the harness and Christopher Albert's original
derivations and their companions. Companions of contributed originals retain
the same unrecorded source terms; the MIT notice does not substitute for missing
third-party licence notices.
